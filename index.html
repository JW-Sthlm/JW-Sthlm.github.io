Public does not always mean open source.

Projects marked MIT licensed are explicitly reusable. Other repositories are available to inspect, learn from, or discuss, but no general reuse right is implied.

The featured repositories have passed a first scan for private links, tenant identifiers, local paths, and common credential patterns. Product-specific Microsoft examples remain where they are useful. If you want to reuse something without a license, ask first.
